Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 June 2026

Corporate BlaBla Decoder (“the app”, “we”) is operated by Odit Teknoloji ve İletişim Hizmetleri Ticaret A.Ş. (“Odit”), Istanbul, Türkiye. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We designed the app to collect as little as possible.

No account, no name

You can use the app without creating an account or giving your name or email. On first launch we create an anonymous device identifier (Firebase Anonymous Authentication) so your settings and streak persist on your device. We do not know who you are.

What we process

  • Text you submit. When you decode a message or compose a rewrite, the text you enter is sent through our servers to our AI provider (OpenAI) to generate the result, then returned to you. This text is processed to provide the feature. Please do not paste sensitive personal data, real names, or confidential information — the app is built for everyday office phrasing, not secrets.
  • Usage counters. Daily counts of how many decode/compose actions you make, to enforce free-tier limits.
  • Preferences. Your commute times and notification settings.
  • Streak. Your daily-ritual streak count.
  • Push token. If you allow notifications, a device push token so we can send reminders.
  • Purchase status. If you subscribe, our payments provider (RevenueCat, via Apple/Google) tells us whether your subscription is active. We never receive your card details.

Service providers

We share the minimum necessary with: Google Firebase (authentication, database, functions), OpenAI (AI text processing), RevenueCat and Apple/Google (subscriptions), and Expo (push delivery). Each processes data only to provide its part of the service.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles. There are no third-party advertising trackers in the app.

Retention & deletion

Settings, counters, and streak are kept while your install exists. You can permanently delete all of it at any time from Profile → Delete account, which removes your user record and anonymous identity. Submitted text is processed transiently to return a result and is not stored as a personal profile by us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the EU GDPR and Türkiye’s KVKK), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data. Because the app is anonymous and offers in-app deletion, you can exercise deletion directly. For other requests, contact us below.

Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will revise the “last updated” date above.
