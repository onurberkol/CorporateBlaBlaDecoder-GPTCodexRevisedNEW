# SEO and ASO launch kit

## Positioning

**Corporate BlaBla Decoder** turns passive-aggressive office messages into clear
meaning and helps people write a reply with deliberate tone. The positioning is
office communication clarity with humour, not “AI writes emails”.

Primary Turkish search themes:

- plaza dili, kurumsal iletişim, iş maili, mail yanıtlama;
- pasif agresif mesaj, toplantı dili, ofis mizahı;
- profesyonel cevap, mail yazma, iş yerinde iletişim.

Do not repeat these mechanically in titles or descriptions. Every page and
listing should lead with a real user benefit.

## Screenshot narrative

Use the existing real-app captures, in this exact sequence:

1. **Plaza dili bugün ne diyor?** - Daily Plaza establishes the distinctive ritual.
2. **Mailin alt metnini gör.** - Decoder shows the core utility and meter.
3. **Cevabının tonunu seç.** - Composer shows deliberate control.
4. **Sonucu paylaşılabilir karta dönüştür.** - Demonstrates the viral loop.
5. **Daha net cevaplar için Köşe Ofis.** - Premium appears after value, not first.

Rules: use screenshots captured from a signed production-equivalent build;
keep headline overlays outside the phone UI; avoid price, awards, rankings,
misleading device frames, tiny type or claims that cannot be demonstrated in
the application.

## Technical SEO implemented

- One Turkish landing page with a unique title, meta description, canonical URL,
  Open Graph/Twitter metadata and visible H1.
- SoftwareApplication JSON-LD matching the on-page product description.
- Semantic sections, descriptive image alt text, crawlable internal legal links.
- robots.txt and sitemap.xml.

Before launch: replace the “yakında” store labels with the real URLs only after
the app records are public, verify the canonical domain, submit sitemap.xml in
Search Console, and create a localized English landing page before targeting
English search.
