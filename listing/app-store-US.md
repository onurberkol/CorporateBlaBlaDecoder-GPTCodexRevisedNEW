### Description (≤4000)
Ever stared at "per my last email" and felt your soul quietly leave your body?

Per My Last Email translates passive-aggressive work messages into the blunt truth underneath — and helps you write your own reply, in exactly the tone you want.

DECODE
Paste any "gentle reminder," "circling back," or "adding [Manager] for visibility" and see what it actually means — complete with a passive-aggressiveness meter and the traps it's hiding.

COMPOSE — THE TONE DIAL
Type what you really want to say. Slide the Tone Dial from Kind → Professional → Surgical → Boss mode and watch your message transform. Copy the perfect reply; it never leaves your phone.

DAILY PLAZA
One shareable office ritual a day — a meeting-bingo, a corporate horoscope, a translation riddle. Keep your streak. You're not the only one who survived that meeting.

BUILT FOR THE GROUP CHAT
Every result becomes a clean, stamped card designed to be screenshotted and shared. No private details — just the universal corporate moment everyone recognizes.

FREE EVERY DAY. CORNER OFFICE UNLOCKS MORE.
Corner Office gives you unlimited decode/compose, every tone (Surgical + Boss), a weekly passive-aggressiveness report, and Tone Memory that learns how your boss writes.

Your real messages stay on your device. We don't want your secrets — just your sense of humor.
