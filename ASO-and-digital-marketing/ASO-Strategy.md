# ASO Strategy — Corporate BlaBla Decoder / Per My Last Email

App Store Optimization playbook for both markets and both stores.
One binary, two localized listings: **TR — Corporate BlaBla Decoder**, **US — Per My Last Email**.

---

## 1. Executive summary

ASO goal: own the long-tail of "corporate / passive-aggressive / work email / plaza dili"
search intent in both stores, and convert that intent with a screenshot set that lands the
joke in under three seconds. Because the product is inherently shareable (every output is a
share card with a QR), ASO and virality compound: shares drive branded searches, branded
searches need a flawless listing to convert.

**Three levers, in priority order:**
1. **Conversion (CRO)** — icon + first 2 screenshots + title. Biggest, fastest ROI. A 1pp CVR lift beats most keyword wins.
2. **Keywords / discoverability** — title, subtitle/short-description, keyword field, description density.
3. **Ratings & velocity** — volume + recency of installs and reviews feed both stores' ranking.

---

## 2. How the two stores rank (and why our metadata differs)

| | Apple App Store | Google Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Keyword sources | App name (30), Subtitle (30), Keyword field (100), IAP names. **Description is NOT indexed.** | App name (30), Short description (80), **Full description (4000) IS indexed**. |
| Keyword rule | No repeats needed; never repeat a word across fields. No spaces in keyword field. | Repetition/density matters (keep natural, ~2–3% for primary terms). |
| Localization | Per-locale keyword fields; can target extra languages to widen index (e.g., add EN keywords on the TR store). | Per-locale listings. |
| Creative test | Custom Product Pages + Product Page Optimization (up to 3 variants). | Store Listing Experiments (A/B). |

**Implication:** on Apple we pack keywords into name + subtitle + the 100-char field and never waste a slot; on Play we additionally weave keywords naturally through the full description.

## 4. Conversion (CRO) — the biggest lever

### 4.1 Icon
The `cb` stamp-red mark. Recognizable, no text, scales to 1× on a busy home screen.
Test variant: solid red vs. paper background. Keep the wordmark out of the icon.

### 4.2 Screenshots (carry 60–70% of the convert decision)
Use the captioned set in `store-assets/`. Ordering principle: **hook → utility → payoff.**
1. **Daily Plaza** — relatable hook ("a new office trap every morning").
2. **Decoder** — the core "aha" (the 96 meter + blunt translation).
3. **Tone Dial** — the wow interaction (dark mode pops in the shelf).
4. **Share card** — the social proof / virality promise.
5. **Corner Office** — monetization, last.

Rules: first 2 must land without context (most users never swipe). Caption = benefit, not feature. Keep one consistent visual system so the brand is recognizable mid-scroll.

### 4.3 Preview video (15–30s) — script
> Cold open on a real-looking email "Per my last email…" → finger pastes it → translation snaps in with the stamp → meter fills to 96 → cut to Tone Dial sliding Kind→Boss, text morphing → end on the share card flying into a group chat → logo + "Decode the office."

Mute-first (captioned), front-load the joke in the first 3 seconds (autoplay preview).

### 4.4 Feature graphic (Play)
Use `feature-graphic/google-play-feature-1024x500-*.png`. Keep text minimal (Play may overlay UI).

## 5. Ratings, reviews & velocity

- **In-app prompt timing:** trigger the native review prompt right after a *delight* moment — the user's 3rd successful decode or first share, never on launch.
- **Target:** 4.6★+ to protect CVR. Respond to every ≤3★ review within 48h (both stores surface developer replies).
- **Velocity:** both stores weight recent installs/reviews. Concentrate paid + creator pushes to create spikes (launch, feature moments) rather than spreading thin.
- **Review seeding (white-hat):** ask power users via push after a streak milestone; never incentivize or buy reviews.

---

## 6. Apple Search Ads & Play keyword harvesting

- **Apple Search Ads:**
  - *Brand campaign* — bid on `per my last email`, `corporate blabla` to defend branded searches driven by shares (cheap, high-intent).
  - *Discovery/Search Match* — let Apple find new terms; harvest winners into exact-match campaigns; feed losers back as negatives.
  - *Competitor* — bid on adjacent corporate-humor / email-tool brands.
- **Play:** use Google Ads (App campaigns) search-term reports to harvest converting queries; feed them into the description and short description.

## 7. A/B testing plan

| Store | Mechanism | First 3 tests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apple | Product Page Optimization (3 variants) + Custom Product Pages (per traffic source) | (1) Screenshot order: Decoder-first vs Plaza-first · (2) Icon: red vs paper · (3) Subtitle wording |
| Play | Store Listing Experiments | (1) Short description hook · (2) Feature graphic with/without card · (3) Screenshot captions |

Run one variable at a time, ≥7 days or until significance, min ~1–2k listing visitors per arm. Roll wins into the default; never test more than store limits allow.

## 8. Measurement & cadence

- **Tools:** App Store Connect (Impressions, Product Page Views, CVR), Play Console (Store listing acquisition), plus a tracker (AppTweak / Sensor Tower / Mobile Action) for keyword ranks and competitor moves.
- **North-star ASO metric:** organic Product-Page-View → Install CVR, and # of keywords ranking top-10.
- **Cadence:** weekly keyword-rank + CVR review; bi-weekly creative iteration; monthly metadata refresh (rotate the keyword bank); quarterly competitor teardown.

### KPI targets (first 90 days, directional)
| Metric | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Branded search CVR | ≥ 50% |
| Category/keyword CVR | ≥ 30% |
| Avg. rating | ≥ 4.6★ |
| Top-10 keywords (per locale) | 15+ by day 90 |

## 9. Competitor landscape (positioning)
Adjacent but not identical: generic AI email writers (Grammarly, "Help me write"), tone-changer utilities, and meme/entertainment apps. **Our wedge:** none of them dare to be funny or mean, and none speak *plaza dili*. We compete on voice, not features — so ASO copy should always lead with the joke and the cultural hook, not "AI."

## 10. 90-day ASO roadmap
- **Days 0–14:** ship localized metadata (both stores, both markets), icon + 5 screenshots + preview video, Apple Search Ads brand campaign live.
- **Days 15–45:** first PPO/Listing experiments (screenshots, subtitle), harvest Search Match terms, push review prompts to streak-holders.
- **Days 46–90:** monthly metadata refresh from harvested keywords, competitor teardown, scale winning Custom Product Pages per channel (TikTok vs Search traffic).
